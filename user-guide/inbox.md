## What is the Inbox?

**Inbox** is the second item under **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It is not a report: it lists only conversations that need a person. On a phone you land on the Inbox when you open Hay; on a desktop you land on the Dashboard.

The headline says how many conversations are waiting for a human ("Nothing is waiting for a human", "3 conversations are waiting for a human") and the line below it shows the longest wait or how many conversations the AI is handling. A badge on the sidebar entry (and the bell in the top bar on phones) shows the waiting count. The page updates live.

## The lists

- **Waiting for a human** - conversations the AI handed off, longest wait first. Each row shows the customer, the AI's summary, the channel and how long they have waited. Click **Take over** to pick one up: the AI stops replying and the conversation opens for you. **Open** just opens it.
- **You're handling** - conversations you have taken over.
- **Teammates are handling** - conversations a colleague has taken over, with **Handled by** their name.

When nothing is waiting the page says **All quiet**.

## Questions for the team

When the AI doesn't know a fact, it can ask your team and keep answering the customer's other questions meanwhile. These appear under **Questions for the team** with what the customer wrote. For each one:

1. Type **Your answer**.
2. Choose how it goes out: **Send as written** (exactly your words) or let the AI pass it on in the customer's language using only what you wrote.
3. Tick **Save to knowledge** to keep the answer for next time.
4. Click **Send answer**, or **Dismiss**.

If nobody answers in time the customer is told the team will reply and the chat moves to **Waiting for a human**. Team members also get a notification and an email with a link to a page titled **A customer is waiting on this question**, so they can answer without opening the dashboard. Business hours and timing are set under **Quick check with the team**; see [Team alerts, pause and business hours](/docs/user-guide/team-alerts-and-hours/).

## Alerts and pausing the AI

- **Alerts on this device** turns on push notifications for this phone or computer. Details, iPhone steps and **Hide customer details on the lock screen** are in [Team alerts, pause and business hours](/docs/user-guide/team-alerts-and-hours/). The same switch is under **Settings** → **My Profile**.
- **Pause AI everywhere** stops every AI reply on every channel. The status reads **AI is replying** or **AI paused**, and **Resume AI** turns it back on. There is also a link to turn the AI off on one channel only.

## Spam

**Spam** (a link at the bottom of the Inbox) lists conversations Hay held as spam. The AI doesn't answer them and they never count toward your bill. Each row shows why it was held (for example **Spam detector**, **Marked by your team** or **Found at close**) and the signals, such as **Many links** or **Repeated text**. Click **Not spam** to release one (the AI then answers it) or **Open** to read it. On a conversation you can use **Mark as spam**. Spam protection for web chat is under **Settings** → **Channels** → **Webchat**.

Two other links on the page lead to **Conversations** (the full list) and **Metrics and reports** (the Dashboard).
