> TLDR: Yes, Hay.chat can issue a full or partial refund on a Shopify order. By default the refund waits until someone on your team approves it, and a request nobody answers within 48 hours goes to your team.

## How a refund works in the chat

A shopper asks for their money back and Hay.chat can issue a full or partial refund on the Shopify order. By default the refund doesn't go through straight away. It waits for a person on your team to approve it, and only then does it run.

Where the request shows up, and how to approve or decline it, is covered on [Action approvals](/docs/user-guide/action-approvals/).

## What if nobody approves?

The request stays open for **48 hours**. If nobody on your team decides in that time, it expires and the chat goes to your team.

## Can small refunds go through without approval?

Not by default. The default approval limit is **0**, which means every refund waits, whatever the amount. If your workspace saved its own approval settings before, it keeps them. The approval settings live under **Settings** → **Assistant** → **Actions**.

## Is a declined refund billed?

Yes. An action sent for approval bills whatever your team decides, so a refund you decline still counts. See [Billing and usage](/docs/user-guide/billing-and-usage/) for what counts and what doesn't.
