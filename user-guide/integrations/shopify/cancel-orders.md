> TLDR: Yes, Hay.chat can cancel a Shopify order. By default it asks you first, so the cancellation waits until someone on your team approves it, and a request nobody answers within 48 hours goes to your team.

## How a cancellation works

When a shopper wants to cancel, Hay.chat can cancel the order in your Shopify store. By default it doesn't do this alone. The cancellation waits for a person on your team to approve it, and it runs once they do.

A cancellation is one of the money moves that wait by default, along with refunds and discounts. How approving works, and where the requests appear, is on [Action approvals](/docs/user-guide/action-approvals/).

This default applies to existing workspaces too. If your workspace saved its own approval settings, it keeps them. You can check yours under **Settings** → **Assistant** → **Actions** before you go live.

## What if nobody approves?

The request waits **48 hours**. If nobody decides in that time, it expires and the chat goes to your team. An expired approval is one of the six reasons Hay.chat hands a chat to a person.

## Is a declined cancellation billed?

Yes. An action sent for approval bills whatever your team decides, declined or not. See [Billing and usage](/docs/user-guide/billing-and-usage/).
