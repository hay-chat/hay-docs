> TLDR: Yes, for answers and hand-off. Hay.chat answers from the website pages and docs you import, follows your playbooks, keeps to your business hours and hands chats to your team, but it can't look up or change orders without a store plugin.

## What Hay.chat can do without a store plugin

If your shop is custom-built or headless, Hay.chat still answers shoppers from the website pages and docs you import. It follows the playbooks you write, keeps to your business hours, and hands chats to your team when it should.

Web chat, the email inbox channel and Instagram DMs don't need a store plugin, so they work on a custom store too.

Business hours are live, so shoppers are told when your team is back. Hand-off works the same as on any other store: the chat goes to a person when the shopper asks for one, a tool fails and needs manual work, the AI's confidence is low, an answer fails its action-claim check, a playbook reaches a **Handoff to human** step, or an approval expires.

## What it can't do

Without a store plugin, Hay.chat can't look up, refund, cancel or change orders.

## How to set it up

1. Import your site and help docs under **Documents** → **Import Document**. Use **Import from Website** to crawl the site or a sitemap and pull in several pages at once.
2. Add the web chat widget under **Settings** → **Webchat**.
3. Set your business hours under **Settings** → **Channels** → **Business Hours**.

Then write a few [playbooks](/docs/user-guide/playbooks/) for the questions you get most.

## If your store runs on a supported platform

Hay.chat has store plugins for [Shopify](/docs/user-guide/integrations/shopify/), [WooCommerce](/docs/user-guide/integrations/woocommerce/), [Magento](/docs/user-guide/integrations/magento/), [Wix](/docs/user-guide/integrations/wix/) and [Nuvemshop](/docs/user-guide/integrations/nuvemshop/), with [Stripe](/docs/user-guide/integrations/stripe/) for payments. If your shop runs on one of these platforms, connect its plugin instead. Each page above covers what the AI can do there.
